CAT (hereinafter: **"the App"**) respects your privacy. This policy details the types of information we collect, how it is used, and the protections in place for your privacy. Use of the App constitutes your full agreement to this policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

- **Personally Identifiable Information (PII):** During registration, we collect data including your name, age, email address, profile picture, and any other details you choose to provide in your biography.
- **Precise Geolocation Data:** To provide the core service of the App, we collect and transmit your precise location (GPS) while the App is in use (or in the background, subject to your device settings).
- **User Generated Content (UGC):** Chat messages, photos, and files transmitted between users are stored on our servers to facilitate the service.
- **Technical Data:** IP address, device identifier (Device ID), operating system type, and crash logs.

## 2. Third-Party Services and Storage (Google Firebase)

The App utilizes **Google Firebase** services for data storage, real-time database management, user authentication, and data analytics.

- Your information is stored on secure cloud servers provided by Google.
- Google's use of data is subject to the Google Cloud Privacy Policy.

## 5. Sharing Information with Third Parties

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes. Information will only be transferred to third parties in the following cases:

## 6. Data Retention and Account Deletion

- Information is retained as long as the account is active.
- **Right to Erasure:** In accordance with Apple policies and international privacy standards (such as GDPR), you may permanently delete your account through the profile settings in the App. Upon deletion, all identifiable information (name, photos, location) will be removed from our active databases within a reasonable timeframe.
