---
title: "Child Safety Standards & CSAE Policy"
---

The CAT (Come And Talk) application is fully committed to maintaining a safe and secure environment for its users.

- **Age Restriction:** The application is intended strictly for users aged **18 and older**. Any use by minors is strictly prohibited.
- **Zero Tolerance for CSAE:** We enforce a zero-tolerance policy regarding any content or activity related to Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE).
- **Enforcement and Reporting:** Accounts or users found engaging in prohibited activity of this nature will be permanently banned from the platform, and relevant details will be forwarded to law enforcement authorities as appropriate.
- **Reporting Objectionable Content:** Encountered suspicious content or behavior that violates safety guidelines? You can report it to us immediately via email at **[cat.service.app@gmail.com](mailto:cat.service.app@gmail.com)**, and we will handle the inquiry as quickly as possible.
